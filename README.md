Objective:   
To develop and manage a virtual project, applying project management principles tailored to 
your specific field of study and working environment. 
Instructions: 
1. Project Selection: - 
Choose a project relevant to your field of study (e.g., developing a mobile app for 
computer science students, creating a marketing campaign for business students, etc.). - 
Submit a detailed proposal that outlines the project objective, scope and expected 
outcomes and also do not forget to include the ESG as mandatory requirements. 
2. Team Formation: - 
Form groups of 5 students in a group. - 
Assign roles based on team members' strengths and expertise (e.g., project manager, 
developer, designer, analyst). 
3. Project Planning: - 
Develop a detailed project plan using project management tools like Trello, Asana, or 
Monday.com. - - 
Define project milestones, deliverables and timelines. 
Identify potential risks and develop mitigation strategies. 
4. Virtual Collaboration: - 
Schedule regular virtual meetings to discuss progress and address challenges. - - 
Utilize tools such as Zoom or Microsoft Teams for meetings and Microsoft Teams for 
ongoing communication. 
Document meeting notes and decisions made during meetings. 
5. Execution and Tracking: - 
Assign individual tasks and responsibilities within the project management tool. - - 
Track progress against the plan and make adjustments as needed. 
Use collaboration software to share documents and feedback.

Prepare a mid-project report summarizing progress, challenges and any changes in 
scope or timelines. - 
Submit a final report at the end of the project detailing accomplishments, lessons learned 
and insights gained. 
7. Presentation: - 
Create a virtual presentation of the project’s outcomes. - - 
Highlight key achievements, innovations and any deviations from the original plan. 
Conduct a Q&A session with peers and faculty. 
Assessment Criteria: - - - - 
Project Planning & Execution: Clarity of objectives, quality of planning and adherence to 
timeline. 
Collaboration & Communication: Effectiveness of teamwork, use of virtual tools and 
communication clarity. 
Problem-Solving & Adaptability: Ability to tackle unexpected challenges and make 
necessary adjustments. 
Presentation & Reporting: Quality and professionalism of reports and presentations, 
insights and lessons articulated.
